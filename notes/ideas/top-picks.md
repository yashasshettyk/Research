# Top 5 ideas from all lists (2026-11-08)
Chosen for M.Tech: one student can finish in about 1 year, low cost, publishable, a real gap.

1. **Home Guardian (Wi-Fi fall + theft alert)**: already designed, unique mix, cheap hardware. MAIN PROJECT.
2. **Scam-call deepfake voice detector for Indian languages**: AI voice-clone scams are rising. Check papers to confirm a Kannada/Indian-language gap.
3. **Prompt-injection shield for AI assistants**: a hot topic, software only.
4. **Offline Kannada small AI on a phone**: shrink a model; useful where the internet is poor.
5. **Which appliance eats my electricity bill?**: splits one meter reading per appliance. Public datasets exist (e.g. REDD, UK-DALE); Indian data is very scarce (iAWE: one Delhi house), a possible gap. See `literature/notes/energy-disaggregation.md`.
