# M.Tech CSE topic options (extendable to PhD) — 2026-11-07

1. **Kannada fake-news detection** — Problem: fake news in Kannada spreads unchecked; tools work only for English. PhD: explainable, multimodal, AI-generated fake news. (Details: project-idea-1.md)
2. **Early crop disease detection on low-cost phones** — Problem: farmers lack quick, offline diagnosis; big AI models don't run on cheap phones. PhD: multi-crop, drone images, pest prediction.
3. **Deepfake voice/call scam detection** — Problem: AI-cloned voices are used in phone fraud in India; few detectors handle Indian languages/accents. PhD: real-time, multilingual, video deepfakes.
4. **Privacy-preserving medical AI (federated learning)** — Problem: hospitals can't share patient data, so AI models stay weak. Federated learning = training a model without moving the data. PhD: secure, fair, multi-hospital models.
5. **Phishing / malicious URL & SMS detection with LLMs** — Problem: new scam messages (incl. regional-language SMS) fool rule-based filters. PhD: adversarial attacks, explainable cyber-security.
6. **Reducing LLM hallucination in Indian-language Q&A** — Problem: chatbots give confident wrong answers, worse in Kannada/Hindi. PhD: trustworthy, fact-checked multilingual LLMs.
7. **Smart traffic signal control with reinforcement learning** — Problem: fixed-timer signals cause jams in Indian cities. RL = AI that learns by trial and reward. PhD: city-wide, emergency-vehicle priority.
8. **Mental-health risk detection from social media text** — Problem: early signs of depression/stress go unnoticed. PhD: multilingual, ethical, explainable support systems.
