# Topic options, set 7: more community-help ideas (2026-11-08)

Shravya asked again for ideas that help the community. These are new and don't repeat sets 1–6 (set 4 was the first community list).

| # | Idea | Who it helps | What we build | Possible gap (to verify with papers) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bus number reader for blind people** | Visually impaired people waiting at bus stops | Phone camera reads the number/route board of an arriving bus and speaks it aloud | Moving bus, night light, Kannada + English boards |
| 2 | **Snakebite helper** | Villagers and farmers bitten by snakes | Photo of snake → venomous or not + nearest hospital with antivenom | Small image data of Indian snakes; must be safe when unsure |
| 3 | **Dyslexia early check for kids** | Children who struggle to read, often labelled "weak" | Short reading/writing game on a phone flags kids who need help | Very little work in Kannada and other Indian languages |
| 4 | **Heatwave alert for outdoor workers** | Construction workers, delivery riders, farmers | Personal heat-risk alert by voice, from weather + type of work | Personal (not city-wide) risk; voice alerts in local language |
| 5 | **Lake shrinking watcher** | Cities losing lakes (e.g. Bengaluru) | Compare satellite images over years to flag lakes being filled or encroached | Small lakes, cloudy monsoon images |

## Recommendation
**#1 Bus number reader.** Needs only a phone, easy to demo, and the hard parts (moving bus, night, Kannada text) are real CSE research.

Home Guardian (main project) also helps the community: elders living alone.
