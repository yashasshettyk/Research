# 20 broader M.Tech CSE topic options — 2026-11-07
(Shravya didn't like topic-options.md; asked for wider variety.)

## Helping people
1. **Indian Sign Language → speech translator** — Deaf people struggle to talk with non-signers; very few ISL tools exist.
2. **Brain-computer interface (EEG)** — Paralysed people can't use devices; brain signals are noisy and hard to decode.
3. **AR indoor navigation for the visually impaired** — GPS doesn't work indoors (hospitals, malls, stations).
4. **Companion robots for elderly care** — Lonely elders need robots that understand emotions and speak local languages.
5. **Personalised AI tutor** — One-style teaching leaves weak students behind; AI can adapt to each learner.

## Nature & environment
6. **Elephant–human conflict early warning** — Elephants enter farms/villages (big issue in Karnataka); detect them early from camera/sound.
7. **Flood & landslide mapping from satellite images** — Rescue teams need damage maps within hours, not days.
8. **Air-pollution forecasting with low-cost sensors** — Cities lack street-level, hour-by-hour pollution forecasts.
9. **Green AI** — Training AI uses huge electricity; make models smaller and energy-efficient.

## Culture & creativity
10. **Reading ancient Kannada palm-leaf manuscripts** — Old manuscripts are decaying; normal text readers (OCR) fail on them.
11. **AI music that follows Indian raga rules** — Music AI ignores raga grammar and sounds wrong.
12. **AI-generated game levels** — Designing game levels by hand is slow; AI can create fun, balanced levels.
13. **Cricket analytics from video** — Detailed shot/bowling analysis needs costly coaches and equipment.

## Future tech
14. **Quantum machine learning** — Today's quantum computers make errors; find learning methods that still work.
15. **Drone swarms for search & rescue** — Many drones overlap or collide; they need smart teamwork.
16. **Self-driving on Indian roads** — Models trained abroad fail with no lanes, cows, autos and potholes.
17. **Digital twin of a smart campus** — A virtual copy to test changes (energy, crowds) before doing them for real.
18. **AI for drug discovery** — Finding a new medicine takes 10+ years; AI can shortlist good molecules fast.

## Security
19. **Bug detection in blockchain smart contracts** — Code bugs have lost millions; find them before launch.
20. **Making AI-written code safe** — AI coding assistants often write insecure code; detect and fix it automatically.
