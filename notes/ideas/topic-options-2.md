# 20 broader M.Tech CSE topics (round 2) — 2026-11-07
Shravya didn't like round 1 (topic-options.md). Varied areas; each has a problem to solve + PhD scope.

1. Brain-computer interface: decode EEG brain signals to type text for paralysed patients — current systems are slow/inaccurate.
2. Sign language to speech: real-time Indian Sign Language translation on a phone — few ISL datasets/tools.
3. Digital twin of a city/campus: virtual copy to predict power/water/traffic problems before they happen.
4. Space/satellite AI: detect floods, deforestation or illegal mining from satellite images quickly.
5. Green AI: cut the electricity/carbon cost of training big AI models without losing accuracy.
6. Quantum machine learning: test if quantum algorithms beat classical ones on small real problems.
7. Blockchain for land/academic records: stop forged certificates and land-record fraud.
8. AR/VR learning: adaptive virtual labs for students who lack physical lab equipment.
9. Assistive tech for the blind: phone app that describes surroundings/reads signs in Kannada.
10. Self-driving on Indian roads: detect potholes, cattle, unmarked lanes — models trained abroad fail here.
11. Drone swarms: coordinate many drones for disaster search-and-rescue without a central controller.
12. Wearable health monitoring: predict heart problems early from smartwatch data on-device.
13. AI music/art generation for Indian classical styles (Carnatic ragas) — current models ignore them.
14. Smart energy grids: predict and balance solar power at home level to avoid outages.
15. Game AI: game characters that adapt to each player's skill so games stay fun and educational.
16. Code-writing AI: find and fix security bugs in AI-generated code automatically.
17. Robotics: low-cost robot arms that learn tasks by watching a human video once.
18. 6G/IoT networks: smart allocation of network bandwidth for millions of connected devices.
19. Emotion-aware tutoring: e-learning system that detects student confusion from face/voice and adapts.
20. Wildlife protection: detect animals near railway tracks/villages from camera feeds to prevent accidents.
