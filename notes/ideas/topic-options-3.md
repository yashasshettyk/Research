# 20 everyday-life M.Tech CSE ideas — 2026-11-07
(Shravya wants ideas that help common people in daily life.)

## Health
1. **Handwritten prescription reader** — People can't read doctors' handwriting → wrong medicine. Phone reads it, explains dose in Kannada, sets reminders.
2. **Calorie counter for Indian food** — Diet apps don't know dosa/thali; diabetics can't track meals. Photo → nutrition estimate.
3. **Fake medicine checker** — Counterfeit medicines are common. Photo of strip/box → genuine or suspicious.
4. **Fall detection for elders using Wi-Fi** — Elders living alone fall unnoticed; cameras invade privacy. Wi-Fi signals sense falls, alert family.
5. **Phone as a hearing aid** — Hearing aids cost ₹20k+. App that makes speech clear in noisy places.

## Food & water
6. **Food adulteration check by phone camera** — Milk/turmeric/fruit adulteration harms health; lab tests are costly.
7. **Drinking water safety check** — Phone reads a cheap test strip → safe or unsafe.

## Money & shopping
8. **Vegetable price forecaster** — Farmers sell too cheap, buyers pay too much. Predict fair mandi prices.
9. **Fake review detector** — Shoppers are fooled by paid fake reviews online.
10. **Simple payment app for elders** — Elders find UPI apps confusing and get cheated. Interface that adapts to the user.

## Travel & safety
11. **Bus arrival time without GPS** — People wait without knowing when the bus will come; use passengers' phone data.
12. **Automatic pothole mapping** — Phone sensors in vehicles detect potholes and report them to the city.
13. **Safest route for women** — Maps show shortest route, not safest (lighting, crowd, past incidents).
14. **Accident auto-alert** — In crashes, help comes late. Phone detects crash, sends location to ambulance/family.

## Home
15. **Which appliance eats my electricity bill?** — One meter shows only total units; AI splits it per appliance (fan, fridge, AC).
16. **Smart waste sorting bin** — People don't separate wet/dry waste; camera sorts automatically.

## Government & paperwork
17. **Government scheme finder by voice** — People don't know which schemes they qualify for. Kannada voice chat finds them.
18. **Loan/insurance document simplifier** — Fine print is confusing; AI explains in simple Kannada and flags hidden charges.
19. **Waiting-time predictor for hospitals/offices** — People lose a whole day in queues; predict best time to go.

## Work
20. **Voice job finder for daily-wage workers** — Plumbers, maids, painters can't use job apps; voice-based matching in local language.

Top picks recommended: #1, #2, #18.
