# Topic options, set 5: rare, little-studied ideas (2026-11-07)

Shravya asked for ideas that "aren't discovered yet" but are interesting and helpful. No one can promise an idea is completely untouched, so these are ideas that are **rarely studied**, especially in India. Novelty must be checked with a paper search before she chooses (no citations yet).

| # | Idea | Problem | What we build | Why it's rare / new |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Child left in school van alert** ⭐ | Children fall asleep and are left locked inside school vans in the heat | A cheap Wi-Fi sensing board in the van detects breathing or movement after the driver locks it, then alerts the driver and parents | Car makers use costly radar; cheap Wi-Fi sensing in crowded Indian vans and autos is barely studied. Reuses her Wi-Fi fall-alert knowledge |
| 2 | **Snake alert at the doorstep** | India has the most snakebite deaths in the world; most happen at night in villages | A small radar plus night camera at the door spots a snake and warns the family | Snake detection at night with cheap sensors is almost unstudied; there is very little data on snakes moving |
| 3 | **Open borewell finder** | Children fall into abandoned open borewells | A drone flies over farms; AI finds open borewell holes so they can be covered | Finding tiny holes from drone images is a hard and new vision problem |
| 4 | **Live-current water warning** | In the monsoon, people die from electric current leaking into rainwater on roads | A cheap sensor on a street pole detects leaking current and warns people and the electricity board | Leak checks exist in rich cities using costly vans; a cheap, AI-based street sensor is rare |
| 5 | **Stampede early warning** | Crowd crushes at temples, festivals and railway stations | Phone/Wi-Fi signals and CCTV detect dangerous crowd "pressure waves" minutes before a crush | Predicting a crush early from phone signals is very new |
| 6 | **Scam call guard in Kannada** | Elders lose savings to "digital arrest" and fake-relative voice scams | AI on the phone listens during a call and warns "This looks like a scam" in Kannada | Big companies cover English; Indian languages and these scam scripts are barely studied |

## Recommendation
**#1 Child left in school van alert.** It saves children's lives, it is rare research, and it reuses the Wi-Fi sensing she already understands.
