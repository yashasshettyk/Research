# 5 fresh M.Tech CSE ideas (2026-11-08)
New ideas, not repeating earlier lists. Each needs only a laptop and public data.

1. **Prompt-injection shield for AI assistants**: AI chatbots and agents can be tricked by hidden instructions in web pages or emails. Build a detector that blocks them.
2. **Handwritten answer-sheet grader**: Teachers spend days correcting papers. AI reads handwritten answers and suggests marks with reasons.
3. **Offline Kannada AI on a phone**: Big AI models need internet and costly servers. Shrink a small model so it answers Kannada questions offline.
4. **Lab-report explainer**: Patients can't understand blood-test reports. A photo of the report gives a simple Kannada explanation and flags values to discuss with a doctor.
5. **Privacy-safe CCTV**: Cameras record everyone's faces. Blur faces but still detect fights, falls or crowding.

Best backup pick: #1, a hot research topic with free datasets and no hardware, so it is easy to publish.
Main project stays Home Guardian (already designed and started).
