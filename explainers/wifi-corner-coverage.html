Will it catch a fall in the corner?

Short answer: usually yes. Wi-Fi does not travel as one thin straight line. It bounces off walls, the floor and the ceiling, so it fills the whole room. But a fall far from the boards gives a weaker signal change, so we place the boards smartly to make every corner safe.
STEP 1

Wi-Fi bounces, like light from a lamp

A person anywhere in the room blocks or changes some of these paths, so the orb "feels" it.

STEP 2

Strongest in the "egg zone"

The zone between the sender and the receiver is shaped like an egg. Movement inside it changes the signal the most. Far corners change it less. Scientists call this the Fresnel zone.

STEP 4

How we make every corner safe

  1. Put the sender inside the room. Walls weaken Wi-Fi, so a router outside gives a weaker signal.
  2. Use 2 receivers (already in our design) on different walls, so every corner is close to at least one.
  3. Do a "corner test" at setup. Lie down for a few seconds in each corner. If the dashboard shows a weak spot, move a board.

Based on the Fresnel zone model: D. Zhang, H. Wang, D. Wu, "Toward Centimeter-Scale Human Activity Sensing with Wi-Fi Signals," IEEE Computer 50(1), 2017.
